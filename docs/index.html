Map. Review. Reuse.

MAPProposes the relationship between each schedule activity or cost line and the WBS.
REVIEWUncertain links are surfaced for a person to confirm. Nothing is quietly assumed.
REUSEConfirmed links carry into the next reporting cycle, with checks for structural change.

The WBS↔LINK Challenge

A synthetic project, three monthly reporting cycles. Map the schedule and cost structures back to the WBS with Excel, SQL, Python, AI, or your own method. Then see how your approach scores.

  1. Download the synthetic project files. The practice set includes its answers.
  2. Map each schedule activity and cost line (plus supporting contractor records) to a WBS element, or mark it LEGIT_ORPHAN, NEW_SCOPE or UNSURE.
  3. Don't guess. UNSURE is an honest answer.
  4. Submit your CSV through a GitHub form. A bot scores it within minutes.
  5. Score: silent errors first, then precision, coverage and time.

A missing mapping creates a question. A confident wrong mapping creates a reporting risk.
