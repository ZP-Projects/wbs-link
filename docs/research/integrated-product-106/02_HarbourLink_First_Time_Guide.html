Two results with different decision histories

The sealed recorded example has 1,357 mapped relationships, with 263 unresolved in June and 264 in July. The independently executed conservative practice retained 1,356 mapped and 266 unresolved in each cycle. These are different professional decisions, not target scores. Your own defensible decisions may differ. The package contains no answer key.

1 Start with June source records

Use the four Cycle 1 files: Owner WBS, contractor schedule, cost forecast and package register. Their source as-of date is 30 June 2026. Planned activity dates are different from a source reporting date. The app’s HarbourLink starter imports these four fictional files without approving them.

2 Confirm the reading and approve the structure

In Data, confirm the source roles and source-column readings. Inspect the Owner Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), its hierarchy, code interpretation and shared-prefix controls. Approve the WBS structure only when its reading is defensible. This first approval establishes the work hierarchy; it does not approve source-to-WBS relationships.

3 Inspect relationships and make actual decisions

Use Project, Crosswalk and Review to inspect original assertions, interpretations and contrary evidence. A source assertion is what a file says, not an individual professional confirmation. Confirm a relationship, choose another supported relationship or keep it unresolved as the evidence warrants. Do not copy the recorded example’s outcomes as an answer key.

4 Approve the reporting position separately

Review the resulting issues, exact Actions, uncertainty and withheld conclusions. Crosswalk approval is the second, separate approval gate. It records the reporting position and attribution; it does not individually certify every source assertion. Enter only a truthful reviewer attribution. The synthetic evaluation does not verify professional identity.

5 Read and retain your June outputs

After approval, open Outputs and download the June Management Report PDF, Premium Workbook and JSON. Read the responsive report. Keep these files for comparison. The report, workbook and JSON must describe the same approved position.

6 Upload July as source revisions

Use only the Cycle 2 schedule, cost forecast and package register as NEXT REVISIONS of the same sources, with source as-of date 31 July 2026. Retain the June Owner WBS and its 30 June 2026 date; do not upload a duplicate Owner WBS. A copy is supplied in the package for context, not as a required revision. Review changed evidence and the resulting continuity classifications before making further professional decisions.

7 Review July and approve again

While July is a draft, June remains the approved reporting position and its Outputs remain available. Retain the June downloads at that point if you have not already done so. Make evidence-based July decisions, then approve the reporting position separately. Inspect July Outputs and compare them with your retained June files. Revision history records the approval basis; a post-July historical-download control has not been established and is not promised here.

For the project-controls reviewer

Customer Version 1/2 is the reporting sequence. The stable mapping reference (MV, the technical MappingVersion identifier) identifies an approved relationship snapshot. The workbook template edition is a separate presentation version. These numbers need not match, especially when an earlier draft was superseded.

Read current relationships separately from retired historical versions and split-target export rows. In the sealed July example, 1,622 current relationships produce 1,623 current target rows; three retired versions bring the history to 1,625 relationship versions and 1,626 all target rows. A relationship with two WBS targets creates an extra row without creating an extra distinct relationship.

09 WBS Explorer answers work-area coverage questions using governed scope membership. 14 WBS Hierarchy follows the hierarchy and counts target rows, including split targets and retired rows unless filtered. Do not compare their numbers as though they count the same population.

Unresolved (UNSURE), scope outside the approved WBS (NEW_SCOPE) and no applicable WBS element (NO_WBS) are distinct recorded outcomes. Do not infer zero cost, missing work, progress or project health from a coverage gap. Financial measures remain withheld where their aggregation basis is not established.
